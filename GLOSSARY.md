# Glossary

## Text bounding box

The blue rectangular area that defines where text can be entered, laid out, wrapped, and aligned.

## Photo focus

The point of a contributor's photo that its frame keeps in view when the frame
crops it. A layout carries a focus for each image and gallery frame, which
applies to every photo placed there. During book review the organizer can give
one photo its own focus, which overrides the layout for that photo alone;
resetting it hands the photo back to the layout.
